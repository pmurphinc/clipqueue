ClipQueue uploads with your own Google API credentials, so your uploads never pass through anyone else's servers. It's a one-time setup, about 5 minutes.

Step A: Create your Google OAuth client {% if has_secret %}✓ done{% endif %}
  1. Open Google Cloud Console and create a project (any name).
  2. Enable the YouTube Data API v3 for that project.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform, click Get started, choose External, and fill in the app name and your email.
  4. Under Audience, add your own Google account as a test user.
  5. Under Clients, create a client with application type Desktop app, then download its JSON file.

Saved only in your ClipQueue data folder. Full guide with troubleshooting: docs/setup-youtube.md.

Step B: Connect your channel

Google will warn that the app isn't verified. That's expected: it's your app. Click Continue.
